Challenge

The Problem

In many rural and tribal educational environments, teachers and students may not share the same language. A teacher may be trained in Hindi or another commonly used language, while students may be more comfortable communicating in their mother tongue.

01
🗣️

Teacher–Student Language Barrier

Teachers and students often speak different languages, making classroom communication difficult.

02
📚

Limited Digital Resources

Educational content is rarely available in regional and tribal mother-tongue languages.

03
💬

Difficulty in Real-Time Communication

Instant translation during live classroom interaction remains a major challenge.

04
✏️

Manual Learning Material Creation

Creating bilingual worksheets and activities manually is time-consuming for teachers.

05
📡

Poor Internet Connectivity

Rural and tribal schools often face unreliable connectivity that limits digital tools.

Our Approach

Our Solution

ShikshaSetu AI is an AI-powered educational solution designed to reduce the communication gap between teachers and students by supporting real-time multilingual communication and mother-tongue-based learning.

Research

Case Study

Our case study focused on understanding the communication gap between teachers and students when they do not share the same language, and exploring how AI can support mother-tongue-based primary education.

Rathinam Technical Campus, Coimbatore Smart Education Artificial Intelligence
A

Objective

  • Understand communication problems caused by language differences
  • Identify primary users
  • Understand teacher and student requirements
  • Explore AI for real-time translation
  • Support mother-tongue-based learning
  • Consider low-connectivity environments
B

Primary Users

Teacher

Needs simple language assistance, quick translation, voice interaction and bilingual teaching content.

Student

Needs familiar language, simple explanations, audio support and accessible educational content.

C

Case Study Scenario

A teacher comfortable with Hindi teaches students who mainly communicate in their mother tongue. ShikshaSetu AI processes the teacher's speech and provides the translated output in the student's selected language. The student can respond in their mother tongue, and the system can translate the response back for the teacher.

D

Key Observations

  • Easy to use
  • Affordable
  • Accessible
  • Suitable for actual users
  • Reliable in low-connectivity areas
  • Suitable for classroom environments
E

Learnings

  • Understanding the user is important before developing technology.
  • Language can become a major barrier to effective education.
  • AI can support accessibility as well as automation.
  • Voice interaction can make educational technology more natural.
  • Offline support is important for areas with limited connectivity.
  • Solutions should be simple enough for non-technical users.

Outcomes

Impact & Benefits

ShikshaSetu AI aims to create meaningful change in primary education through accessible multilingual communication.

Educational Impact

Students can receive explanations in a language they are more comfortable with.

Social Impact

Reduce communication barriers between teachers and students from different linguistic backgrounds.

Economic Impact
